\frohmantitle{The Frohmanian Tethered Metriplectic World Model in Lean~4: Canonical Bracket Splitting, Encoding Obstructions, and the Newton Potential}

\begin{wipbox}
\textbf{Draft --- tracks build in progress.} This repository formalizes a \emph{model}: the tethered metriplectic system, which adds a symmetric tether bracket and cubic damping of peak vorticity to 3D vorticity dynamics. It proves nothing about unmodified Navier--Stokes ($\kappa=0$) and does not resolve the Clay Millennium problem.
\end{wipbox}

\begin{abstract}
We report a Lean~4/Mathlib~\cite{Lean4,Mathlib2020} formalization (0 \texttt{sorry}; axioms only \lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, \lean{Quot.sound}) of the author's tethered metriplectic model (in the sense of~\cite{Morrison1986}). Proved unconditionally: the unique antisymmetric/symmetric splitting of a bilinear bracket (\lean{split_exists_unique}); a non-circular canonicity theorem forcing the metric bracket to the form $c\ip{\Pi_u x,\Pi_u y}$ (\lean{canonical_metric_bracket}); a uniform vorticity ceiling for the comparison inequality $M'\le CM^2-\kappa M^3$; a log-loss replacement for the stretching hypothesis giving a ceiling when $\kappa>4C_{\mathrm{abs}}$; several encoding counterexamples; the Newton-potential identity $\Delta(N\star g)=-4\pi g$ on $\R^3$ for $g\in C^2_c$; and $\curl\BS\,\omega=\omega$ for smooth, compactly supported, divergence-free $\omega$ (\lean{curlBiotSavartTest}). Conditional and open items are listed by their exact Prop names.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Metriplectic dynamics~\cite{Morrison1986} combine a Hamiltonian (antisymmetric) bracket with a dissipative (symmetric) bracket. This paper documents a Lean~4 formalization of the author's tethered metriplectic model of three-dimensional vorticity dynamics, in which a symmetric tether bracket adds cubic damping of the peak vorticity. We establish the algebraic structure of the brackets (Section~\ref{sec:brackets}), the comparison-ODE ceilings and their dependence on the stretching hypothesis (Section~\ref{sec:ceiling}), several obstructions arising from the encoding (Section~\ref{sec:enc}), and the analytic facts about the Newton potential and the Biot--Savart law on $\R^3$ needed for the helicity results. The model is not the Navier--Stokes system, and no statement about unmodified Navier--Stokes is made.

\section{Naming}
\lean{Pi_u} is the author's own name (manuscript of 31 May 2026): the projection off $\spn\{u\}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Piu}\Pi_u x = x-\frac{\ip{u,x}}{\norm{u}^2}\,u.\end{equation} All other identifiers defined in this repository are \emph{coined in this build} (marked \coined), including \lean{canonical_metric_bracket}, kept by the author's choice and flagged as coined. Modules under \file{FrohmanianSymplecticTether/} and \file{TetherMayForm/} are ported verbatim from earlier repositories.
\section{Manuscript framing}
The May/June 2026 manuscript introduces a tethered Lyapunov functional $S_\varepsilon$ and reduces regularity to the comparison ODE
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cmp}y'=Cy^2-\kappa''y^3\end{equation}
for the peak vorticity, through transport cancellation, viscous dissipation at the maximum, a H\"older--Sobolev gate, and Young absorption at $\varepsilon=\kappa/4$ (Proposition~2). Within the model, the cubic term comes from the tether. The manuscript's hypothesis (H5), a non-circular majorant for the vorticity, is the genuine gap; for $\kappa=0$ it is the Clay core. Here $\kappa$ has units of time and breaks Navier--Stokes scaling, which is why the model is distinct from NS. \emph{Normalization of $\kappa$:} where the Lean code fixes $\kappa=3/2$ (\lean{CalderonZygmundConstant3D}, defined as $(3/(8\pi))\cdot4\pi$), this is a convention; it is not a computed Calder\'on--Zygmund operator norm and not derived from a Conley--Zehnder index. The ceilings hold for every $\kappa>0$.
\section{Brackets and canonicity (\texttt{Bracket.lean}, \texttt{Canonical.lean})}\label{sec:brackets}
\begin{theorem}\lean{split_exists_unique}\coined: every bilinear bracket is uniquely an antisymmetric part plus a symmetric part.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{canonical_metric_bracket}\coined: on a real inner product space, a bilinear form $B$ that is symmetric (C1), degenerate along $u$ (C2: $B(u,\cdot)=0$) and invariant under every linear isometry fixing $u$ equals
\[B(x,y)=c\,\ip{\Pi_u x,\Pi_u y}.\] The hypotheses do not mention $\Pi_u$. \lean{canonical_metric_bracket_sign}\coined: if $B(x,x)\le0$ then $c\le0$. \lean{Pi_u_form_symm_degen}\coined: the forced form satisfies C1, C2.\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Scope:} pointwise at one state; does not force the $|\omega|^2$ weight, the value of $\kappa$, or C3. Also proved: \lean{energy_conserved}, \lean{energy_const_along}, \lean{entropy_production}, \lean{gen_entropy_eq}, \lean{entropy_monotone_along}\coined.
\section{Ceiling and stretching (\texttt{Ceiling.lean}, \texttt{AuditFixes.lean})}\label{sec:ceiling}
\begin{theorem}\lean{uniform_ceiling}\coined: if $M'\le CM^2-\kappa M^3$ and $\kappa>0$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ceiling}M(t)\le\max\bigl(M(0),\max(C/\kappa,0)\bigr)\qquad(t\ge0).\end{equation} Also \lean{absorbing}, \lean{ceiling_antitone_kappa}, \lean{kappa_zero_no_ceiling}\coined{} (at $\kappa=0$, $1/(1-t)$ satisfies $M'\le M^2$ and blows up).\end{theorem}
\paragraph{Correction.} The hypothesis \lean{StretchingCZ} (a pure $y^3$ bound on stretching at the maximum) is \emph{false as a sup-norm bound} for Navier--Stokes: the known bound carries a logarithmic loss. Results using it are relabeled as model-only. Its replacement, consistent with the known estimates in form, is \lean{StretchingLogLoss}~$d\,C_1\,C_2$\coined: \[St\le C_1y^3+C_2y^3\log(1+y).\]
\begin{theorem}\lean{maxpoint_rate_logLoss}\coined: under \lean{EnergyIdentity}, \lean{StretchingLogLoss}, \lean{ViscousAtMax}, \lean{TetherFeedback}, \lean{CrossControlled},
\[y'\le\Bigl(C_1+\tfrac{\kappa}{4}+\tfrac{4C_2^2}{\kappa}\Bigr)y^2-\Bigl(\tfrac{\kappa}{4}-C_{\mathrm{abs}}\Bigr)y^3,\]
so with \lean{uniform_ceiling} a uniform ceiling follows whenever $\kappa>4C_{\mathrm{abs}}$ (Young absorption at $\varepsilon=\kappa/4$).\end{theorem}
\section{Encoding obstructions (\texttt{ChoiceFree}, \texttt{HelmholtzObstruction}, \texttt{TestClass})}\label{sec:enc}
\begin{itemize}
\item \lean{not_streamFunctionRepModGrad}\coined: \lean{StreamFunctionRepModGrad} fails for every $\omega$ --- representatives are determined only a.e.; uniqueness must be stated almost everywhere (open).
\item \lean{arnoldBracketOf_antisymm}, \lean{entropyCasimir_arnold}\coined: a choice-free Arnold bracket~\cite{Arnold1966} and a Casimir criterion.
\item \lean{not_curl_biotSavart_eq}\coined: $\operatorname{curl}(\mathrm{BiotSavart}\,\omega)=\omega$ fails for the point spike in the current encoding; \lean{helicity_casimir_of_curlBiotSavart}\coined{} is conditional on \lean{CurlBiotSavartOn}.
\item \lean{IsTestOrbit}\coined{} (smooth, compactly supported, div-free); \lean{not_isTestOrbit_pointSpike}, \lean{helicity_casimir_test}\coined{} (conditional on \lean{CurlBiotSavartTest}, discharged in \lean{helicity_casimir_test_closed}, Section~7); \lean{integrableOn_ball_norm_rpow_neg}\coined.
\end{itemize}
\section{Newton potential on $\mathbb{R}^3$ (\texttt{NewtonKernel}, \texttt{RegularizedKernel}, \texttt{NewtonLaplacian})}
Proved: \lean{scalarLaplacian_newtonRadial} ($\Delta(1/|y|)=0$, $y\ne0$), \lean{contDiff_newtonPotential}, \lean{hasFDerivAt_newtonPotential} (differentiation under the integral); for $N_\varepsilon=(|y|^2+\varepsilon^2)^{-1/2}$: \lean{scalarLaplacian_regNewton} ($\Delta N_\varepsilon=-3\varepsilon^2(|y|^2+\varepsilon^2)^{-5/2}$), \lean{regNewton_le}, \lean{tendsto_regNewton}, \lean{integral_neg_scalarLaplacian_regNewton} ($\int-\Delta N_\varepsilon=4\pi$), \lean{tendsto_regPeak_approxIdentity} (all \coined). The regularized route avoids spherical Green identities, which Mathlib lacks.
Step 3 is closed (\file{NewtonLaplacian}): \lean{integral_mul_scalarLaplacian_eq} ($\int K\,\Delta g=\int \Delta K\,g$ for $K\in C^2$, $g\in C^2_c$, by Mathlib integration by parts, no boundary terms), \lean{regNewton_conv_laplacian} ($N_\varepsilon\star\Delta g=(\Delta N_\varepsilon)\star g$), \lean{tendsto_regNewton_conv} (the $\varepsilon\to0$ limit, dominated by $N|h|$), \lean{scalarLaplacian_newtonPotential} ($\Delta(N\star g)=N\star\Delta g$), and
\begin{theorem}\lean{scalarLaplacian_newtonPotential_eq}\coined: for $g\in C^2_c(\mathbb{R}^3)$ and $N(y)=1/|y|$, $\Delta(N\star g)(x)=-4\pi g(x)$ for every $x$.\end{theorem}
\section{Biot--Savart on the test class (\texttt{BiotSavartNewton})}
With $G_j(t)=t_j/|t|^3$ (\lean{gradKer}\coined{} at $\varepsilon=0$):
\begin{theorem}\lean{newtonPotential_deriv_eq}\coined: for $h\in C^1_c$, $N\star\partial_jh=-\int G_j(t)\,h(x-t)\,dt$. (Regularized identity \lean{regNewton_conv_deriv}, then $\varepsilon\to0$ on both sides; the right side is dominated by $|t|^{-2}|h|$.)\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Step 5]\lean{biotSavart_eq_curl_newtonVec}\coined: for \lean{IsTestOrbit}~$\omega$, $\mathrm{BiotSavart}\,\omega=(4\pi)^{-1}\operatorname{curl}(N\star\omega)$ pointwise, $N\star\omega$ taken componentwise (\lean{newtonVec}).\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Step 4]\lean{curl_curl_newtonVec}\coined: for \lean{IsTestOrbit}~$\omega$, $\operatorname{curl}\operatorname{curl}(N\star\omega)=4\pi\omega$, using symmetry of second derivatives (\lean{fderiv_fderiv_swap}), $\operatorname{div}(N\star\omega)=N\star\operatorname{div}\omega=0$, and the step-3 theorem.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{curlBiotSavartTest}\coined: \lean{CurlBiotSavartTest} holds, i.e.\ $\operatorname{curl}\mathrm{BiotSavart}\,\omega=\omega$ for every smooth, compactly supported, divergence-free $\omega$. Consequently \lean{helicity_casimir_test_closed}\coined: helicity is a Casimir of the Arnold bracket at such $\omega$, given the explicit argument $D\,S=u$.\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Scope:} this is the classical identity on the smooth compactly supported class only; it says nothing about the dynamics. The G\^ateaux derivative of helicity is treated in Section~\ref{sec:hel}.
\section{Helicity on the test class (\texttt{HelicityGateaux})}\label{sec:hel}
\noindent\emph{Correction to a definition.} \lean{IsSmoothOrbit} previously used $\texttt{ContDiff}\ \mathbb{R}\ \top$, which in current Mathlib means analytic; a compactly supported test orbit was then $0$ and the earlier test-class statements were vacuous. It now uses $C^\infty$ (the order $(\top:\mathbb{N}_\infty)$ coerced to $\texttt{WithTop}\ \mathbb{N}_\infty$); the class contains every $\operatorname{curl}\psi$ with $\psi\in C^\infty_c$ (\lean{isTestOrbit_curlOrbit}\coined).
\begin{theorem}\lean{biotSavart_selfAdjoint}\coined: for continuous compactly supported $a,b$, $\int\langle Ba,b\rangle=\int\langle Bb,a\rangle$ (Fubini with the majorant $|x-y|^{-2}|a(y)||b(x)|$, kernel symmetry, triple product).\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{helicity_gateaux}\coined: with $\mathcal H(\omega)=\int\langle B\omega,\omega\rangle$ (\lean{helicity}\coined), $2B\omega$ is a G\^ateaux representative of $\mathcal H$ on the test class.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{curl_eq_of_isGateauxRepOn}\coined: two $C^1$ representatives of the same functional on the test class have equal curl. Hence \lean{helicity_casimir_of_rep}\coined: if $D\,S$ is any $C^1$ representative of $\mathcal H$, then $\operatorname{curl} D\,S=2\omega$ and $S$ is a Casimir of the Arnold bracket \lean{ArnoldBracketOf}; no hypothesis on the value of $D\,S$.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{streamFunctionRepModGradOn_of_exists}\coined: \lean{StreamFunctionRepModGradOn} follows from one $C^2$ kinetic-energy representative $\psi$ with $\operatorname{curl}\psi=u$ and the Poincar\'e lemma \lean{CurlFreeIsGradient}\coined; the curl half of uniqueness is \lean{kineticEnergy_reps_curl_eq}\coined.\end{theorem}
\section{Poincar\'e lemma and the kinetic-energy representative (\texttt{PoincareR3}, \texttt{KineticEnergyRep})}
\begin{theorem}\lean{curlFreeIsGradient}\coined: every $C^2$ curl-free field on $\mathbb{R}^3$ is the gradient of a $C^2$ function.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{decays_newtonPotential}\coined: if $g$ is continuous, compactly supported and $\int g=0$, then $|N\star g(x)|\le C(1+|x|)^{-2}$. Derivatives of $C^1_c$ functions and the coordinates of a divergence-free test field have mean zero (\lean{IsTestOrbit.integral_coord_eq_zero}\coined).\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{integral_pairing_curl_symm}\coined: \[\int\ip{a,\curl b}\dd x=\int\ip{\curl a,b}\dd x\]for fields with decaying coordinates and first derivatives. Hence, with $\psi=(4\pi)^{-1}N\star\omega$ (\lean{keStream}\coined), $\int\langle B\omega,B\varepsilon\rangle=\int\langle\psi,\varepsilon\rangle$, so $\psi$ is a $C^\infty$ G\^ateaux representative of the kinetic energy with $\operatorname{curl}\psi=u$ (\lean{keStream_isGateauxRep}\coined).\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{streamFunctionRepModGradOn}\coined: \lean{StreamFunctionRepModGradOn} holds unconditionally on the test class.\end{theorem}
\noindent\emph{Smoothness audit.} \lean{instSmooth} in \file{Instance.lean} stated analyticity where $C^\infty$ was meant (still true, now stated as $C^\infty$). \lean{contDiffAt_newtonRadial} states analyticity of $1/|x|$ off $0$; that is true and proved, so it is left as is.
\section{Maximizer data from a vorticity field (\texttt{MaxPointDanskin}, \texttt{EntropyCasimirArnold})}
\begin{theorem}\lean{danskin_upper_dini}\coined: if $f,\partial_t f$ are jointly continuous on $\mathbb{R}\times K$, $K$ compact, then for some maximizer $x_\star$ of $f(t,\cdot)$ on $K$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, $\sup_K f(t+h)\le f(t,x_\star)+h(\partial_t f(t,x_\star)+\varepsilon)$ for small $h>0$; i.e.\ $D^+\sup_K f(t)\le\partial_t f(t,x_\star)$. \lean{vorticity_danskin_upper_dini}\coined\ applies it to $|\omega|^2$.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{sup_mul_deriv_eq_inner}\coined: if $Y=\sup|\omega|$ is differentiable at $t$, then $YY'=\omega\cdot\partial_t\omega$ at every global maximizer. At such a point $\omega\cdot(v\cdot\nabla)\omega=0$ (\lean{inner_convective_eq_zero_of_max}\coined) and $\omega\cdot\Delta\omega\le0$ for $C^2$ fields (\lean{inner_laplacian_nonpos_of_max}\coined).\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{maxPointRealization_of_field}\coined: for a solution of \lean{ModelVorticityPDE} with $\nu\ge0$, $\omega(t)\in C^2_c$, $C^1$ in time, and $Y=\sup|\omega|>0$ differentiable, \lean{MaxPointRealization} holds with \lean{EnergyIdentity}, \lean{ViscousAtMax} and \lean{CrossControlled} discharged. The remaining hypotheses are \lean{FieldStretchingBound}\coined\ and \lean{FieldTetherFeedback}\coined. Running the ceiling argument from the one-sided Dini bound alone is open.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{arnoldCasimirField_helicity}\coined: $2u$ satisfies \lean{ArnoldCasimirField}\coined\ ($\forall a$, \lean{ArnoldBracketOf} $\omega\,a\,(2u)=0$) on the test class. This is the clean form of \lean{EntropyCasimir} for the concrete bracket (\lean{entropyCasimir_of_arnoldCasimirField}\coined). The choice-based form \lean{EntropyCasimirChoice}\coined\ is kept and marked not provable as stated.\end{theorem}
\section{The ceiling from the Dini bound (\texttt{DiniCeiling})}
\begin{theorem}\lean{ceiling_of_upper_dini}\coined: if $M$ is continuous on $[0,\infty)$, $M(t+h)\le M(t)+h(R(t)+\varepsilon)$ for small $h>0$, and $R(t)<0$ whenever $M(t)>B$, then $M(0)\le B$ implies $M\le B$ on $[0,\infty)$.\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{field_uniform_ceiling}\coined: take a solution of \lean{ModelVorticityPDE} with $\nu\ge0$, $\kappa>0$, $C^2$ in space, $C^1$ in time, and support in a fixed compact set. Under \lean{FieldStretchingBound} and \lean{FieldTetherFeedback}, $\sup|\omega(t)|\le\max(\sup|\omega(0)|,C/\kappa,0)$ for all $t\ge0$. No differentiability of $\sup|\omega|$ is assumed; its continuity is proved (\lean{sup_continuous_of_uniform_support}\coined).\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}\lean{fieldTetherFeedback_cubicTether}\coined: for $T=-\kappa|\omega|^2\omega$ (\lean{cubicTether}\coined), \lean{FieldTetherFeedback} holds. Hence \lean{field_uniform_ceiling_cubicTether}\coined\ needs only \lean{FieldStretchingBound}. For a general tether it does not follow.\end{theorem}
\section{Log-loss stretching with the cubic tether (\texttt{LogLossCeiling})}
\begin{theorem}\lean{field_uniform_ceiling_logLoss_cubicTether}\coined: take a solution of \lean{ModelVorticityPDE} with the cubic tether, under the regularity and uniform-support hypotheses of \lean{field_uniform_ceiling}. Assume the named Prop \lean{FieldStretchingLogLoss}\coined, $\omega\cdot(\omega\cdot\nabla)u\le C_1Y^3+C_2Y^3\log(1+Y)$ at maxima. Then $\sup|\omega(t)|\le\mathrm{ceiling}(Y_0,\,C_1+\kappa/4+4C_2^2/\kappa,\,3\kappa/4)$ for every $\kappa>0$.\end{theorem}
This is a reduction, not an unconditional result. The Calder\'on--Zygmund estimate is not formalized here. Its known form has $\log^+\bigl(\norm{\omega}_{C^\alpha}/Y\bigr)$, a higher norm that is not controlled uniformly in time, so it would not close the argument. The $Y$-only log loss is a strictly stronger assumption.
\section{Status}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Proved unconditionally:} \lean{split_exists_unique}, \lean{canonical_metric_bracket} (+ sign, symm/degen), energy/entropy structure, \lean{uniform_ceiling}, \lean{maxpoint_rate_logLoss} (as an implication from its listed Props), the obstructions above, the Newton potential identity (steps 1--3), \lean{curlBiotSavartTest} (steps 4--5), Biot--Savart self-adjointness, the Poincar\'e lemma \lean{curlFreeIsGradient}, \lean{streamFunctionRepModGradOn} on the test class, $D\mathcal H=2u$ and the helicity Casimir of the concrete Arnold bracket on the test class (\lean{helicity_casimir_of_rep}), the scalar instance \lean{instance_global_regularity}, the Danskin bound \lean{danskin_upper_dini} and the maximum-principle lemmas.
\item \textbf{Conditional on named Props:} \lean{model_uniform_ceiling} (\lean{TetheredMaxRate}); \lean{model_global_regularity} (\lean{TetheredMaxRate}, \lean{ModelContinuation}); \lean{model_global_regularity_of_realization} (\lean{MaxPointRealization}, whose fields include \lean{StretchingCZ} --- false as an $L^\infty$ NS bound); velocity-tether energy/entropy (\lean{KineticEnergyCurlIdentity}, \lean{EntropyCasimir}); helicity Casimir on the test class given $D\,S=u$ (\lean{helicity_casimir_test_closed}; no longer conditional on \lean{CurlBiotSavartTest}).
\item \textbf{Open:} \lean{EntropyCasimir} for the choice-based \lean{FunctionalDerivative} bracket (not provable as stated); deriving the $|\omega|^2$ weight, $\kappa$ and C3 from C1--C3; for the field-level realization (\lean{maxPointRealization_of_field}): \lean{FieldStretchingBound} (or \lean{FieldStretchingLogLoss}; the CZ estimate is not formalized, and its $C^\alpha$ form would not close the ceiling), \lean{FieldTetherFeedback} for tethers other than \lean{cubicTether}, and existence and regularity (uniform compact support) of actual solutions. Differentiability of $\sup|\omega|$ is no longer needed (\lean{field_uniform_ceiling}); and, for unmodified NS, the (H5) majorant, i.e.\ the Clay core. Neither the Clay problem nor NS regularity is claimed.
\item The type named \lean{T3} is $\mathbb{R}^3$, not the torus.
\end{itemize}
